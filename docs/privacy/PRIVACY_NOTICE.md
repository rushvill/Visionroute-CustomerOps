# VisionRoute Customer Ops — Privacy notice

**Version:** 2026-08-02  
**Controller:** VisionRoute (small business operating this Customer Ops service)

This notice explains how we handle personal data in the VisionRoute Customer Ops app. It is written to align with common requirements under the **EU GDPR** (if applicable) and the **Philippines Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)**. It is an operational notice for the product — not legal advice. Have counsel review before public launch.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| Name, email, phone, company/name | Signup, contact, account setup |
| Login username, password (hashed) | Secure sign-in |
| GPS unit labels, plate/attach point, unit IDs | Show your vehicles/units in the portal |
| Subscription / promo / invoice / payment records | Run the service and track bills |
| Support tickets (if used) | Help with issues |
| Hashed IP / user-agent on signup | Abuse prevention |

We do **not** use this app as a live GPS map. Tracking maps (if any) are a separate VisionRoute service.

## Legal bases (summary)

- **Contract / service setup** — process signup and provide your account  
- **Legitimate interests** — secure the service, prevent fraud, keep business records  
- **Consent** — recorded when you accept this notice at signup  
- **Legal obligation** — where tax or regulatory rules require retention  

## Cookies / sessions

We use an **essential session cookie** (`vr_ops_session`) so you stay signed in. It is HttpOnly and not used for advertising. No third-party ad cookies in this app.

## Who we share with

Staff who operate VisionRoute Customer Ops. Hosting providers (e.g. database / app host) under contract. We do not sell personal data.

## Retention (initial)

| Record | Retention |
|--------|-----------|
| Signup requests | ≥ 1 year after decision, or anonymize |
| Active accounts & units | While account is active + business need |
| Invoices / payments | Per tax/accounting needs (often years) |
| Sessions | Expire by idle/absolute timeout |
| Privacy requests | Until handled + audit trail |

## Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request:

- **Access** to personal data we hold about you  
- **Correction** of inaccurate data  
- **Erasure** (“delete my data”) where legally allowed  
- **Restriction / objection** in limited cases  
- **Portability** of data you provided, where applicable  

Submit a request in the app (**Privacy** page or customer portal) or email the address VisionRoute publishes for privacy. We aim to respond within **30 days** (or local legal deadline).

## Children

This service is for adult customers and businesses, not directed at children.

## Changes

We may update this notice. The version date above and the in-app signup consent version identify which notice you accepted.
